Three treatments, one diagram

All three draw the same slice of the object map and share the mockup's palette, so the only thing you're choosing is treatment. Whatever you pick gets applied to all five panels.

The slice on purpose exercises the hard part: a Space containing Lessons (solid line — delete the parent, the child goes too), a Contribution pointing at a Lesson (dashed — delete the target and it orphans, never disappears), a Material drawn faded because it is removable without breaking its Lesson, and a Contribution carrying its author.

A · Product-native

looks like the app
Why it winsReads like the product; Doc 3 could lift tokens straight out of it. Familiar the second you open it.
Why I'd hesitateA diagram that looks like real UI gets read as a screen spec. Doc 1 is about objects, not screens — this treatment quietly invites the wrong question ("so that's the Lesson card?"). Card chrome also competes with the lines, which are the actual content.

B · Schematic

recommended
Why it winsThe lines are the loudest thing on screen, which is correct — the relationships are the content. Monospace labels read as object names, not UI copy, so nobody mistakes it for a screen. Corner ticks mark the containing object. Inverts cleanly to white for thesis figures later.
Why I'd hesitateCold. It looks like documentation, not like your product — if you want the doc set to feel like Learnstation, this is the least on-brand of the three.

C · Editorial

type-led, minimal
Why it winsCalmest of the three and the easiest to read at a glance — which matters when five panels sit on one page. Closest to the "calm competence" voice.
Why I'd hesitateWithout boxes, the containment boundary gets soft. Panel 1 has ten objects, not five — at full density this treatment risks becoming a word cloud with lines, and the one thing the page must never be ambiguous about is what contains what.

Not offered on purpose: a cosmic/galaxy treatment. Doc 1 locks the theme to visuals only, with the galaxy map as the one fully themed screen — a themed foundations diagram would spend that budget in the wrong place and blur the map's distinctiveness.

Palette is identical in all three (#0b0d14 · #141824 · #6c5ce7 → #00d2d3) so the choice is treatment only. A hybrid is available: B's line grammar with A's card chrome — say the word and I'll mock that too.
