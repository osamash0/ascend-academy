01

The object map

what exists, and what owns what

Two lanes, because they answer different questions and Doc 2 will have to put them on different screens. Above the line is the Space's content. Below it is yours — a Membership, your Progress, your Notes — none of which the Space owns. The Material is drawn dashed and faded because deleting it leaves the Lesson working: grounding and the tutor read extracted Lesson content, never raw files. The Contribution's three cyan lines fan to its three possible anchors, exactly one of which it picks. ⊗ on the Space is the locked rule that keeps trying to come back: a Space never contains a Space.

02

Two axes, both labels

why hierarchy is a query, not a folder

The left stack answers who owns it, the right stack answers what it's about, and they never meet — a Space can sit in Marburg and be Computer Science, or sit in no Universe at all and still be Computer Science. The bottom row is the whole argument for labels in one picture: the identical Space resolves into two different filters at the same time. A folder tree physically cannot do that, which is why grouping is metadata resolved at read time and the level list is capped at three.

03

Origin × Grounding

two properties, genuinely independent

Four filled cells is the point — if any cell were impossible, the two properties would be one property wearing two names. Origin asks who made it. Grounding asks whether it traces back to the nominated truth set. The fifth state is the one most likely to be lost in implementation, so it's drawn as an absence rather than buried in a rule: with grounding off, the grid doesn't render at all.

04

Guided and Open

one difference: who may publish

Deliberately near-identical columns, because after v1.12 the modes really are near-identical — the single real difference is the gate on the left. In Guided, a Member reaching for the path is blocked and their work lands in the community section. In Open, the same person publishes a Lesson straight into the path, carrying a Community badge and their name. Everything downstream keeps working in both: Concepts get extracted, progress records, the map lights. Promotion exists on both sides, and the switch touches only future permission — which is what makes "switchable at any time" honest rather than a slogan.

05

States

every name here becomes a screen state

These names matter past bookkeeping: each one becomes a state a screen has to render, and writing them down now stops them being reinvented under different names in Docs 2 and 5. Two carry decisions rather than mechanics — Archived keeps progress but earns no XP, and Orphaned surfaces to the Owner and the author, because work that quietly disappears is the thing that makes contributors stop contributing.

What's deliberately not here: screens, navigation, XP amounts, and anything cosmic. Screens are Doc 2 and Doc 5, XP is Doc 8, and the theme is locked to visuals with the galaxy map as the one fully themed screen — a themed foundations diagram would spend that budget in the wrong place.

Ascent, galaxy map and Rank live in other docs and are absent on purpose, not missing.
