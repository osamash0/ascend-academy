This appendix holds five diagrams that the body refers to.

\thesisfigure{f4-use-case}
  {Actors and goals as implemented. Student goals are the v4 app; creating
   and publishing a Space is gated on ownership, not role; professor and
   admin goals remain in the classic console. The shaded goal is built but
   switched off in production.}

\thesisfigure{f5-domain-model}
  {Conceptual domain model. Analysis-level vocabulary, deliberately distinct
   from the physical database schema.}
  {fig:domain-model}

\thesisfigurepage{f9-er-core}
  {Core physical schema, part 1: identity, Spaces, content and parsing --- 19
   of the 36 core tables, out of 79 tables defined in the migrations
   (distinct \code{CREATE TABLE} names, one of them the default partition of
   the event table). The dashed line has no database-enforced foreign key.}
  {fig:er}

\thesisfigurepage{f9b-er-learning}
  {Core physical schema, part 2: the 17 learner-facing tables (practice, Luna
   sessions, notes, gamification), attached to grey stubs of part-1 tables.
   Dashed lines have no database-enforced foreign key; two are polymorphic
   \code{space\_id} text links.}
  {fig:er-learning}

\thesisfigure{f15-security-arc}
  {Privilege-escalation hardening in two phases: five steps over six
   migrations from January to June 2026, then four steps over five migrations
   from October to November 2026. Steps 1 and 3 each opened a hole that a
   later step closed, and the arc has no final state.}
  {fig:security-arc}
