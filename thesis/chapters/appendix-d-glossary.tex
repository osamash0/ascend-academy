\chapter{Terms}
\label{app:glossary}

The terms of the system and of the evaluations, in alphabetical order, with
the section that introduces each.

\begin{description}
  \item[answer check] A second model answers a generated quiz question from
    its cited page without seeing the key; a different answer disputes the
    key (\cref{sec:c6-lifecycle}).
  \item[Ask] The reading view's action that answers a learner's question
    about the open page (\cref{sec:stakeholders}).
  \item[ask bar] The question field on a Space's home screen; it answers from
    all Lessons of the Space through retrieval (\cref{sec:retrieval}).
  \item[arm] One scripted Space in the study with scripted readers, with a
    fixed mix of personas (\cref{sec:c5-method}).
  \item[author, the] The writer of this thesis.
  \item[Bradley--Terry model] A model of pairwise comparisons in which each
    method has a strength and its expected score against another is its share
    of the two strengths (\cref{sec:parser-bench}).
  \item[checkpoint] A question that a model writes for the pages of one idea
    while a learner reads; not the deck quiz.
  \item[comparison] In the benchmark, one batch of duels among a fixed set of
    methods; there were four (\cref{sec:parser-bench}).
  \item[corrected lower bound] The lower end of a method's bootstrap
    interval, Bonferroni-corrected for testing ten methods at once. A method
    passes H1 if it lies above 1000.
  \item[current extraction] The production extraction, PyMuPDF's plain-text
    call; the anchor of the benchmark at 1000 points.
  \item[deck plan] A deterministic outline of a deck made without a model
    call: page roles, sections, and boilerplate (\cref{sec:c4-deck-plan}).
  \item[deployed] Running in production; compare \emph{in the code}.
  \item[detector] A rule over the events of one Lesson that produces
    findings (\cref{sec:c5-aggregation}).
  \item[duel] One blind comparison of two methods' outputs for one page,
    answered left, right, same, or both unusable.
  \item[Explain] The reading view's action that has a model explain the open
    page (\cref{sec:stakeholders}).
  \item[figure block] A region of a page that PyMuPDF reports as an image or
    a drawing.
  \item[figure-carried page] A page with fewer than 150 footer-free
    characters whose figures cover at least 5\,\% of it; the routing rule
    sends it to a vision model (\cref{sec:routing}).
  \item[finding] A detector's statement about one page: its kind, the page,
    and a severity.
  \item[floor] The rule that nothing is said about a page that fewer than
    five readers have viewed (\cref{sec:c5-aggregation}).
  \item[generating model] The model that wrote a quiz question. The answer
    check runs on a different one.
  \item[H1] The benchmark's hypothesis that at least one method is rated
    above the current extraction, tested on the corrected lower bounds.
  \item[idea] One thing the material teaches, usually a run of consecutive
    pages that a model groups.
  \item[in the code] Reachable at the pin, whether or not deployed.
  \item[lecturer] The person who wrote the material, as distinct from the
    owner who uploads it.
  \item[Lesson] One uploaded document in a Space.
  \item[Library] A person's own uploads.
  \item[Luna] The assistant of study sessions.
  \item[older interface] The first interface of the system, with screens per
    course, lecture, and professor; still in the code, no longer reached by
    learners (\cref{sec:vision}).
  \item[owner] The person who made a Space and uploads its material, and who
    receives the owner's view.
  \item[owner's view] The screen that shows an owner how the material of a
    Space is read (\cref{sec:c5-door}).
  \item[page] One page of a Lesson's document, a slide or a sheet.
  \item[parse receipt] The record a parse writes: for each page its route,
    the prompt and its version, the provider, the model, the tokens, and the
    cost, and the counts of quiz questions kept, repaired, and dropped.
  \item[pin, the] Revision \code{\thesisCommit} of 6~November 2026, the code
    this thesis describes (\cref{sec:scope}).
  \item[pipeline v1 to v5] The numbered versions of the parsing code
    (\cref{tab:lineage}); unrelated to the v4 interface.
  \item[points] A rating on the benchmark's scale: 1000 plus 400 times the
    decimal logarithm of a method's strength relative to the current
    extraction, so that 400 points are a ratio of 10:1 in expected score.
  \item[probe] The 16 hand-written pairs of a correct and a wrong answer key
    used to measure verifiers (\cref{sec:c6-method}).
  \item[reach] The word (none, few, or enough) that the owner's view gives
    instead of a count of readers.
  \item[reader] A person who reads. The readers of a page are the members
    other than the owner who viewed it.
  \item[reading view] The screen in which a Lesson is read, with three views
    of each page: the original, an explanation, and the plain text.
  \item[refusal gate] The ask bar's rule that refuses before any model call
    when no retrieved page reaches a similarity of 0.65
    (\cref{sec:anti-hallucination}).
  \item[rehearsal] A short mixed test that a model writes from a Space's
    pages; computable tasks in it are graded by code
    (\cref{sec:c6-grading}).
  \item[routing] The decision, page by page, between text extraction and a
    vision model (\cref{sec:routing}).
  \item[Space] A place where a group reads the same material; stored as a
    course.
  \item[Space engine] The aggregation that turns a Space's events into the
    owner's view (\cref{sec:c5-aggregation}).
  \item[sweep] A job that runs every ten minutes, closes stuck parse runs,
    and fails runs that have been queued for six hours.
  \item[uploader] The user whose upload started a parse; a file is parsed at
    most once per uploader and pipeline version (\cref{sec:idempotency}).
  \item[verifier] A step that computes, validates, filters, or drops
    generated content.
  \item[vision model] A model that reads the rendered image of a page
    instead of its text.
\end{description}
