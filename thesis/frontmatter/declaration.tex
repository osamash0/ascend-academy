\paragraph{Use of AI tools.}
Together with a co-developer I built the system, and this thesis covers the
parts that \cref{sec:scope} lists. AI coding agents generated most of its
code under my direction (\cref{sec:method-note}). I reviewed their changes,
by reading the changes that mattered, running the tests, and checking the
result in the running system, and I decided what went into the system. A
language model helped me draft, revise, and review the text of this thesis.
I checked and decided everything.
