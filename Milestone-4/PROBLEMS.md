Every **unresolved** problem from the consolidated audit, ranked by severity. Fixed and retracted findings are excluded — see [`APP_AUDIT_REPORT.md`](APP_AUDIT_REPORT.md) for those, and for methodology, contradictions between the source audits, coverage gaps and merge order.

**133 open problems** · 🔴 14 blockers · 🟠 40 high · 🟡 54 medium · 🔵 25 low

`M` ids come from the five reconciled audits (mostly prod, browser-observed). `R` ids are source-level, with `file:line`. `F` ids come from the 2026-08 branch consolidation — repo plus direct production-database probing, no browser. Ids are stable across all documents — **never renumber them**.

Only the two `F` **blockers** appear below. The other seven (`F2`–`F7`, `F9` — GDPR erasure and export gaps, the missing RLS-composed test, a dead index, one-directional type drift, two unreliable tests, duplicate storage objects) live in [`APP_AUDIT_REPORT.md`](APP_AUDIT_REPORT.md) Part 8 rather than being duplicated here.
